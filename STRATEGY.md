# Strategic Vision 2030: The Coastal-Highland Link

## 1. Regional Integration
The goal is to create a seamless economic zone where the highlands of Sanaag and the coast of Saaxil function as a single market unit.

## 2. Development Phases
* **Phase 1 (Current):** Xarqo to Dhuur-Cilaan (120 KM) - Focus on drainage and earthworks.
* **Phase 2 (Planned):** Expansion towards Berbera and deeper into the Sanaag heartlands.

## 3. Long-term Impact
* **Urbanization:** Controlled growth of transit towns like Dhuur-Cilaan and Xarqo.
* **Education & Health:** Using the road to deploy mobile clinics and improve school access for nomadic communities.

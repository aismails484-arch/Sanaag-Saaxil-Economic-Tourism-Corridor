# Phase 1 Technical Specification: Xarqo – Dhuur-Cilaan

## 1. Project Specifications
* **Route Distance:** 120 Kilometers.
* **Administrative Jurisdiction:** Ceel-Afweyn District, Sanaag.
* **Primary Corridor:** Sanaag-Saaxil Integration Axis.

## 2. Infrastructure & Engineering Requirements
The 120 KM stretch from Xarqo to Dhuur-Cilaan has been assessed for its unique terrain requirements:

### A. Hydrological Management (Drainage Systems)
* **Non-Bridge Terrain:** The route does not require large-scale bridge structures.
* **Culvert Systems:** Implementation of reinforced box culverts and pipe culverts is the priority to manage seasonal water run-off (togga).
* **Erosion Control:** Strategic drainage placement to prevent road-base degradation during the Gu and Deyr rainy seasons.

### B. Terrain Analysis
* **Soil Composition:** Primarily limestone and rocky terrain typical of the Golis Range foothills.
* **Earthworks:** Significant leveling and grading are required to ensure the 120 KM path is safe for heavy commercial vehicles carrying livestock and minerals.

## 3. Resource Mapping (Economic Potential)
The road directly impacts the extraction and transport of:
* **Resins:** High-quality Frankincense (Maydi/Beeyo) from the Golis range.
* **Livestock:** Facilitating the transit of camels and small ruminants to Berbera.
* **Mining:** Enabling exploration and transport of minerals from the Ceel-Afweyn hinterlands.

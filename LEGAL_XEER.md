# Institutional Framework & Legal/Xeer Governance

## 1. Regulatory Compliance
All infrastructure planning within the Sanaag-Saaxil Corridor adheres to the national standards of transport and environmental regulations.

## 2. Customary Law (Xeer)
* **Land Use:** Coordination with local pastoral communities in the Ceel-Afweyn district to ensure right-of-way without disrupting traditional grazing patterns.
* **Community Ownership:** Utilizing the 'Xeer' system to manage resource sharing (water and gravel) during the construction phase.

## 3. Institutional Oversight
* **Lead Agency:** SPH Global Research (Strategic Planning).
* **Collaboration:** Partnering with local district authorities and regional administrations to ensure project sustainability.

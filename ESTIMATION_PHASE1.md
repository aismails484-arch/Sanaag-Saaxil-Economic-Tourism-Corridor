# Comprehensive Financial Estimation: Phase 1 (Xarqo – Dhuur-Cilaan)
## Project Path: 120 KM | Sector: Infrastructure Development

### 1. Financial Strategy Overview
The total realistic valuation for an all-weather standard road for this 120 KM stretch is estimated at **$15,000,000 USD**. However, Phase 1 is structured with an initial mobilization budget of **$3,000,000 USD** to commence critical earthworks and hydrological management.

### 2. Detailed Budget Breakdown (Phase 1 Mobilization)

| Activity | Technical Description | Estimated Cost (USD) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Site Mobilization** | Transport of heavy machinery (Bulldozers, Graders) to Xarqo/Ceel-Afweyn. | $350,000 |
| **Clearing & Earthworks** | Initial road-bed clearing and terrain leveling for the 120 KM stretch. | $1,050,000 |
| **Hydrological Works** | Construction of high-priority reinforced box culverts in major flood zones. | $850,000 |
| **Rock Blasting** | Specialized excavation in the Golis Range (Xabkaha/Fuleenka) sections. | $300,000 |
| **Engineering Supervision** | Topographical surveys, GIS mapping, and onsite technical monitoring. | $250,000 |
| **Local Coordination** | Security, community engagement, and environmental mitigation. | $200,000 |
| **TOTAL (Phase 1)** | | **$3,000,000** |

### 3. Engineering & Cost Realism
* **Topography Impact:** Due to the mountainous nature of the Golis range, the cost per kilometer for a full standard road is approximately **$125,000/KM**. 
* **Phase 1 Goal:** To establish a functional, drained, and graded gravel road that enables immediate transit for trade and tourism while seeking further investment for full paving.
* **Drainage vs. Bridges:** By prioritizing over 150+ drainage points (culverts) instead of a few large bridges, we ensure the entire 120 KM remains passable during the rainy seasons.

### 4. Long-Term Valuation (Total Project)
* **Phase 1 (Initial works):** $3,000,000
* **Phase 2 (Base course & Paving):** $12,000,000
* **Estimated Total Completion:** **$15,000,000**

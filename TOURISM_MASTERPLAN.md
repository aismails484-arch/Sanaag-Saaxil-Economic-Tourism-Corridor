# Tourism Development Framework: Sanaag Highlands

## 1. Key Destinations
This corridor unlocks access to some of the most significant natural landmarks in the Horn of Africa:
* **Golis Range (Silsilada Golis):** Focusing on the scenic beauty of the Xabkaha and Fuleenka mountains.
* **Climate Tourism:** Leveraging the cool mountain air as a retreat for domestic and international tourists.

## 2. Infrastructure for Eco-Tourism
* **Viewpoints:** Strategic locations along the 120 KM route for scenic viewing.
* **Conservation Zones:** Protecting the endemic flora (Boswellia trees) while allowing controlled tourism access.
* **Cultural Heritage:** Integrating local pastoral history into the tourist experience.

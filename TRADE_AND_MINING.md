# Trade, Mining, and Export Analysis: Sanaag-Saaxil Corridor

## 1. Commercial Significance
The 120 KM stretch from Xarqo to Dhuur-Cilaan acts as a primary artery for the export economy of the Sanaag region.

## 2. Key Export Commodities
* **Frankincense & Resins:** Sanaag is the global heart of Frankincense (Maydi and Moxor). This road reduces the transit time from the Golis mountains to Berbera Port, preserving the quality of the resin.
* **Livestock Trade:** The route facilitates the movement of 'Berbera Grade' livestock. Improved road conditions reduce animal weight loss during transport.
* **Mining & Minerals:** The Ceel-Afweyn district and surrounding areas are rich in mineral potential. This infrastructure framework supports future extraction and logistics for the mining sector.

## 3. Economic Logistics
* **Cost Reduction:** Transitioning from rugged tracks to a structured road will lower vehicle maintenance costs by an estimated 40%.
* **Port Integration:** Direct linkage to the Berbera Economic Zone (BEZ).
